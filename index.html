01 Biomedical · AI · 3D

heart3D

An ECG is twelve flat shadows of one arrow swinging through the heart. heart3D rebuilds that arrow in 3D from real hospital recordings, animates it beat by beat, and lets two AI models read the trace and show which part of the heartbeat drove their answer.

21,799
real ECGs (PTB-XL)
0.92
AUC, 5 diagnosis groups
0.96
AUC, heart-attack wall
  • Reconstructs the 3D heart vector with the Kors matrix and plays it in three.js.
  • A gradient-boosted model on measurements and a 1D ResNet on raw signals, both close to published benchmarks.
  • Shades the likely heart-attack wall on an anatomical heart, plus a normal-vs-abnormal comparison page.

A learning tool, not a medical device.

Python · NumPy · scikit-learn · PyTorch · signal processing · three.js

02 Mobile app · API · Dashboard

FarmConnect

A mobile-first marketplace and community for Kenyan farmers: ask growers in other regions what worked, see what's selling and for how much, trade directly, and build a trust score through honest listings and completed orders.

  • Expo / React Native app: feed, marketplace, community Q&A, orders, profiles, push notifications.
  • Node / Express API on MongoDB: JWT and SMS-code sign-in, rate limiting, moderation and reporting.
  • M-Pesa payments for listing boosts and sponsored posts, with buyer protection.
  • Admin dashboard in React + TypeScript: analytics, revenue, broadcasts and partner accounts.

Expo · React Native · TypeScript · Node.js · Express · MongoDB · Socket.io · M-Pesa · Sentry

03 Quant research · Automation

Gold trading bot & research

I tested five strategies on 20 years of gold prices, with real spreads, financing costs and unseen years held back. None had a real edge; the one that looked great over two years lost 69% over twenty. Then I built the bot anyway, safety first: demo-only lock, 1% risk sizing, daily loss limit, kill switch and 17 tests.

Python · pandas · MetaTrader 5 API · Dukascopy & FRED data · unittest
