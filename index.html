01 Biomedical · AI · 3D

heart3D

An ECG is twelve flat shadows of one arrow swinging through the heart. heart3D rebuilds that arrow in 3D from real hospital recordings, plays it on a real heart shape with the electrical wave sweeping across it, and lets two AI models read the trace and show which part of the heartbeat drove their answer.

21,799
real ECGs (PTB-XL)
0.93
AUC, 5 diagnosis groups
19
CT scans behind the 3D heart
  • Reconstructs the 3D heart vector with the Kors matrix and plays it in three.js.
  • A real heart in 3D: the average adult heart from 19 CT scans, with an activation wave timed to each ECG through the atria, then the ventricles. It also models bundle branch blocks and shades the likely heart-attack wall.
  • Live mode streams an ECG into the viewer as if from a bedside device, with vitals and both AI models updating as it plays.
  • Redesigned as an ECG instrument: a paper chart theme, a dark monitor theme, and a true 12-lead layout.
  • A gradient-boosted model on measurements and a 1D ResNet on raw signals, both close to published benchmarks.

A learning tool, not a medical device.

Python · NumPy · scikit-learn · PyTorch · signal processing · three.js

03 Quant research · Automation

Gold trading bot & research

I ran 33 strategies through one honest simulator on 20 years of gold prices: textbook indicators, news-day trades, a friend's "smart money" method, and seven 1-minute scalps. Real spreads, financing costs, a luck correction, and the last two years sealed until the end. Almost nothing survived: the scalps all lost about 100% after costs, and the SMC method lost 38%. One rule, the inside-day breakout, passed its sealed exam, but it failed on seven other markets, so it runs on a demo-only bot with a kill switch and 23 tests.

Python · pandas · NumPy · MetaTrader 5 API · Dukascopy & FRED data · unittest

Behaviour and genetics
